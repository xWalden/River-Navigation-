Safety Briefing▼

NC paddleboard rules
  • SUP = a vessel outside swim zones.
  • Adults: carry USCG Type I/II/III PFD. Under 13: wear it.
  • Carry a whistle; white light if near dusk.
  • Wear your leash on moving water.
Swift Creek specifics
  • Slow Coastal Plain stream — Class I with deadfall and strainers.
  • Avoid 24–48 hr after heavy rain.
  • Banks are mostly private — take out at road bridges.
  • File a float plan with a trusted contact.
